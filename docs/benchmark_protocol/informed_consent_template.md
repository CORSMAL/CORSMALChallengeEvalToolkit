## **CORSMAL Benchmark – Volunteer Consent Form**

**Purpose of the Study**  
You are invited to participate in a robotics handover experiment conducted by the participating team as part of the CORSMAL Benchmark Qualification Phase. The purpose of the study is to evaluate robot performance during human–robot handovers.

**What You Will Be Asked to Do**  
You will perform a series of handover tasks involving a cup with different fillings. Your hands will be recorded on video during the task.

**Data Collected**
- Video recordings of your **hands only** during the task  
- Optional, non‑identifying demographic information (age group, gender, dominant hand, experience level)

**How the Data Will Be Used**
- Videos will be shared with the CORSMAL organisers for verification of the team’s submission.  
- Videos will **not** be published or redistributed without your explicit consent.  
- Videos will be deleted by the organisers after verification (within 30 days).  
- Submission data may be used in academic publications describing the benchmark results.

**Voluntary Participation**
Your participation is voluntary. You may withdraw at any time without penalty.

**Confidentiality**
- No personal identifying information will be collected.  
- Videos will be anonymised before submission.  
- Consent forms will be stored securely by the participating team and will not be shared with organisers.

**Your Rights**
- You may refuse to answer any question.  
- You may request deletion of your video from the team’s storage at any time.  
- You may decline to have your video used for public demonstrations or publications.
